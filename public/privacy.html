Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Radulator ("we," "our," or "us") operates the website radulator.com (the "Site"), which provides free, evidence-based medical calculators for healthcare professionals and medical learners. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our Site.

By using radulator.com, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Site.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you submit our website feedback form (powered by Formspree), we may collect the following information. This form is used solely to gather voluntary feedback for improving the Site and is not a calculator data channel:

  • Name (required) — to identify the feedback source
  • Email (required) — to respond to your feedback
  • Feedback Type (required) — Bug Report, Feature Request, or General Feedback
  • Calculator (optional) — to identify the relevant calculator
  • Message (required) — to receive your detailed feedback

Important: The feedback form is used solely to collect user suggestions, bug reports, and general feedback to improve the website. Submitting feedback is voluntary and sends the form fields to Formspree. Please do not include patient information, protected health information (PHI), or other identifiers in feedback messages. Do not place patient information in feedback text or URLs. Calculator inputs and results are processed locally in your browser and are not sent by the calculator application to our servers.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

Application analytics are paused for this privacy baseline. Radulator does not load an analytics SDK, send application analytics events, or record calculator searches, inputs, results, copies, prints, navigation, or feedback outcomes for analytics.

Hosting providers and network intermediaries may still receive unavoidable request and security logs, such as IP address, timestamps, request paths, referrer information, and browser or device metadata, according to their own operations and retention practices. These logs are not application analytics and are not controlled by the calculator code.

2.3 Cookies and Local Storage

Radulator uses browser local storage to save your UI preferences, including favorite calculators, recently used calculators, dark mode setting, and whether you have acknowledged the medical disclaimer. This data is stored entirely on your device, is never transmitted to our servers, and contains no patient or clinical information. You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your feedback and improve our calculators
  • To fix bugs, improve performance, and develop new features
  • To display relevant advertisements (if applicable)
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We use the following third-party services:

  • Formspree — Feedback form submission processing. Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Pages — Website hosting. Privacy Policy

Advertising

We may display advertisements through Google AdSense or similar services. These services may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Each of these services may collect additional information as described in their respective privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

5. Data Retention

Feedback form data (name, email, message) is retained in Formspree for as long as necessary to respond to your feedback and improve the website. This data is used exclusively for website improvement purposes.

You may request deletion of feedback data from the feedback processor as described in Section 8.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • Our Site is served over HTTPS, encrypting data in transit
  • We use reputable third-party service providers with their own security measures
  • Our codebase is maintained in a private repository
  • All calculators process data client-side only — no calculation data is transmitted to any server

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

Radulator is intended for use by healthcare professionals and medical learners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

8.1 For All Users

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data

8.2 California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect and how it is used
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information)
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights

8.3 European Economic Area Residents (GDPR)

If you are in the EEA, any processing of feedback you voluntarily submit is handled for the stated feedback purpose. You may have additional rights including data portability, the right to restrict processing, and the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

9. Do Not Track Signals

Application analytics are paused, so there is no application analytics setting for a Do Not Track signal to change. Hosting and network logging practices remain outside the calculator application.

10. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and maintained on servers located outside your state, province, or country where data protection laws may differ. Formspree processes voluntarily submitted feedback on its service, which may involve international transfers described in its privacy policy.

11. Medical Information Disclaimer

Radulator provides medical calculators for educational and informational purposes only. The calculators are not a substitute for professional medical judgment, diagnosis, or treatment. Calculator processing occurs locally in your browser; the calculator application does not send these values to our servers.

All calculator inputs and results, including patient-reported outcome scores, are processed entirely within your browser and are not saved by the application as persistent form state. Closing or refreshing the page clears the application's current form state, but is not a guarantee of secure erasure from your device or browser. Copied results, downloaded CSV files, printed reports, and browser-managed copies may remain under your control. CSV exports are generated locally and downloaded to your device.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Your continued use of the Site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.

13. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, wish to exercise your privacy rights, or need to report a concern, please contact us through our feedback form.
