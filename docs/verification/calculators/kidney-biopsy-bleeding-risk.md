## Current state → required state

The app implements the 2026 refitted major-bleeding equation with six predictors: age, BMI derived from kg/cm, platelets in ×10⁹/L, preprocedure hemoglobin in g/L, greatest target-kidney length in cm, and native versus allograft. It reports probability, BMI, outcome, scope, safety limits and an overprediction warning above 25%. It does not infer procedural fitness or prescribe antithrombotic changes, prophylaxis or monitoring duration.

The owner read the full runtime, math/browser tests, all three cited papers' methods/results/discussion, and the refit equation and units on supplement p. 5 (also rendered/visually inspected). The implementation matches the 22 published equation terms. Existing source-derived coverage is substantial and must be reused, not rebuilt.

Required supported function is the published refit probability for adult diagnostic image-guided percutaneous native/allograft kidney biopsy, with explicit limitations. It must not be presented as a risk model for kidney masses, implantation/open/nonpercutaneous procedures, children, minor bleeding, or a protocol decision. The 2026 paper externally validates the earlier model, then refits on the combined cohorts; it does not independently validate the resulting refit on a new external cohort. Formula reproduction and clinical transportability are distinct.
